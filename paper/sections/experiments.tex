\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:experiments:setup}

\paragraph{Model pair.}
We transfer from Qwen2-1.5B to Qwen2.5-1.5B. The two checkpoints share
architecture (28 layers, hidden size 1536, grouped-query attention), tokenizer,
and vocabulary, and we verify before every run that 1{,}000 WikiText-2
passages tokenize to identical ids under both. The version bump retrains the
base on a larger and differently weighted corpus, so the pair isolates
representational drift from every confound of architecture, width, and
vocabulary.

\paragraph{Tasks.}
We choose three tasks on which a LoRA adapter changes the model substantially,
so that the oracle--naive gap is large and the fraction recovered is
measurable. \textbf{GSM8K}: grade-school math, trained on question--solution
pairs and scored by exact match of the final number in a greedy completion
(500 test problems). \textbf{AG News}: four-way topic classification cast as
generation of the label word after an instruction, scored by exact match
(1{,}000 test articles). \textbf{SAMSum}: dialogue summarization, scored by
the perplexity of the reference summary conditioned on the dialogue, with the
loss restricted to summary tokens (500 test dialogues). Training and
evaluation use the same prompt template, and no test example is seen in
training.

\paragraph{Adapters.}
Every adapter is a rank-16 LoRA ($\alpha=32$, dropout $0.05$) on the
$q$, $k$, $v$, and $o$ projections of all layers, trained for 400 optimizer
steps at an effective batch of 16 sequences of up to 512 tokens with learning
rate $2\times10^{-4}$. The source adapter is trained on Qwen2-1.5B; the
oracle uses the identical recipe and data on Qwen2.5-1.5B.

\paragraph{Anchors.}
Anchors are 2{,}000 WikiText-2 training passages truncated to 256 tokens,
a corpus disjoint from all three tasks. From the non-pad positions we keep
16{,}384 tokens per module, the same rows for both models, and fit every map
from those activations alone.

\paragraph{Conditions and metric.}
We compare five conditions per task: \emph{naive} transfer (the source adapter
loaded unchanged, the operation of \citealp{lin2025finetuningtransfer}),
\emph{Cross-LoRA}~\citep{xia2025crosslora} at its published truncation rank
$k=320$, our method with orthogonal \emph{Procrustes} maps, our method with
\emph{ridge} maps at $\lambda=1$, and the retrained \emph{oracle}. The headline
metric is the fraction of the oracle--naive gap a transfer method recovers,
$(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{method}}-\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{naive}})/(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{oracle}}-\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{naive}})$,
oriented so that $100\%$ means matching the oracle and $0\%$ means no better
than naive for every metric. Because a default can favour either method, we
also sweep $\lambda\in\{0.1,1,10,100\}$ and $k\in\{64,160,320,640\}$ and
report each method at its best setting (Table~\ref{tab:sweeps}).
